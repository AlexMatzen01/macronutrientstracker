CALCULATION

How the math works

BMI note

For adults, BMI is weight in kg divided by height in meters squared. CDC treats BMI as a screening measure, not a diagnosis. For ages 2–19, CDC uses age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles instead of adult BMI categories.

DATA & PRIVACY

Your data is portable.

MacroTrack uses localStorage only. Nothing is sent to a server by this app.

SEED EXPORT

One long numeric string

The seed encodes the app's JSON data as decimal bytes. It is portable, but it is not encrypted. Treat it like a copy of your private data.

JSON BACKUP

Download or restore

JSON is the easiest format to inspect or back up. Importing replaces the current profile and logs with the selected backup.

RESEARCH NOTES

Where the calculations come from.

The app deliberately uses ranges and estimates rather than presenting any formula as an exact measurement.

ENERGY ESTIMATION

Mifflin–St Jeor

The adult resting metabolic rate estimate uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. A systematic review found it was more reliable than several common alternatives, while also noting meaningful individual error.

MACRONUTRIENTS

National Academies

The general adult planning ranges are informed by the Dietary Reference Intakes framework, which uses acceptable macronutrient distribution ranges rather than one “perfect” split.

National Academies →
SPORTS NUTRITION

Academy / DC / ACSM

Sports-focused recommendations emphasize meeting energy and carbohydrate needs, adequate protein, hydration, and individualized planning based on the sport and training load.

PubMed →
EXERCISE PROTEIN

ISSN position stand

The International Society of Sports Nutrition position stand supports roughly 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day of protein for most exercising people, with context-dependent individualization.

BMI

CDC

Adult BMI categories apply to adults 20 and older. For ages 2–19, CDC uses sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles. In both cases BMI is a screening tool, not a medical diagnosis.

DESIGN CHOICE

No “cut” or “bulk” mode

This version focuses on fueling, performance, and tracking. It does not generate automatic weight-loss or weight-gain calorie deficits/surpluses.
